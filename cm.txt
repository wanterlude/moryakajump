Move the content tables into src/ and drop a duplicate perk

src/game/content.ts holds nine independent tables: biomes, bird kinds, run quests, the
perk rarity scale, weights and all 48 perk definitions. The frame catalogue stays behind
because FRAMES is built from EXTRA_FRAMES, which are declared later in the bundle and are
not available at the top of the IIFE where modules are injected.

The new tests found a real bug on the first run: two perks shared the id magnet2 with
different hints but identical mods, so PERK_BY_ID kept one and the offer could show the
other's text. The shadowed copy is removed; the surviving card now tells the truth.

Eight new tests cover biome palettes, bird parameters, quest ids and rewards, rarity
weights summing to one, rarity order consistency, unique perk ids and filled neutral
modifiers. 52 tests total, tsc clean, golden frames unchanged.
